# Chapter 2 Lab: Responsible AI Principles and Scenario Application

**Estimated time:** 12 minutes

## Exercise

Responsible AI scenario drill: analyze 10 real-world AI scenarios (hiring tool, loan approval, medical diagnosis, chatbot) and identify which of the six principles are at risk or being violated, using Microsoft's Responsible AI checklist as a reference

## Key takeaway

Responsible AI questions present a scenario and ask which of the six principles (fairness, reliability and safety, privacy and security, inclusiveness, transparency, accountability) is violated or should guide the solution—look for keywords like 'bias' for fairness, 'explain decisions' for transparency, and 'who is responsible' for accountability.
