# Chapter 1 Lab: Common AI Workloads and Business Scenarios

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Microsoft Foundry portal exploration: navigate AI Services catalog, identify workload categories, match 5 business scenarios to correct AI workload types using the Foundry quick-start templates gallery

## Key takeaway

The AI-901 exam tests your ability to match six workload types (computer vision, NLP, speech, document intelligence, generative AI, agents) to specific business scenarios by identifying keywords like 'extract structured data' for document intelligence or 'autonomous decisions' for agents.
