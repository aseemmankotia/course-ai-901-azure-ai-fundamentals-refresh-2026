## Chapter 2: Responsible AI Principles and Scenario Application — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Which Responsible AI principle is violated when an AI system treats different demographic groups inequitably despite similar inputs? | Fairness. The system must treat all people and groups equitably. Exam tip: Look for scenarios with disparate outcomes for different groups (gender, race, age) - even if the same rules apply to everyone, unequal results indicate fairness violations. |
| 2 | What are the THREE required technical controls for Privacy and Security in AI systems handling sensitive data? | 1) Encryption at rest and in transit, 2) Access controls/principle of least privilege, 3) Data minimization/avoiding storing unnecessary sensitive data. Exam tip: All three must be present - just having one isn't enough. |
| 3 | Fairness vs Transparency - which principle is about explaining HOW decisions are made vs ensuring decisions are EQUITABLE? | Transparency = explaining HOW (understanding decision process). Fairness = EQUITABLE outcomes (treating groups fairly). Exam tip: If question asks about explaining or understanding, choose Transparency. If about biased outcomes or disparate treatment, choose Fairness. |
| 4 | What does Accountability require beyond just monitoring AI system performance? | Human responsibility assignment. Specific people/roles must be designated as responsible for AI decisions and outcomes, with processes for human review and intervention. Exam tip: Accountability = who is responsible (people), not what the system does. |
| 5 | An AI model trained on 90% data from one group performs poorly on another group. Which Responsible AI principle is violated and what's the fix? | Fairness is violated due to biased training data. Fix: Collect representative/balanced training data and retrain model. Exam tip: Imbalanced training data → Fairness problem. Solution is always fixing the data, not just documenting the bias. |
| 6 | Reliability and Safety principle - what does 'handle errors gracefully' mean in exam scenarios? | AI system should: 1) Provide fallback responses when uncertain, 2) Not fail catastrophically, 3) Perform consistently under normal conditions. Exam tip: If system gives wrong answers or fails unpredictably → Reliability issue. |
| 7 | Which Responsible AI principle specifically addresses designing for people with disabilities or different abilities? | Inclusiveness - AI systems should empower and engage ALL people regardless of physical abilities, cognitive capabilities, language, or other characteristics. Exam tip: Accessibility features = Inclusiveness, not Fairness. |
| 8 | Data minimization - which Responsible AI principle does this support and what does it mean? | Privacy and Security. Data minimization = collect and store only the minimum data necessary for the AI system's purpose. Don't store full credit card numbers, SSNs, or other sensitive data unnecessarily. Exam tip: Less stored data = better privacy. |
| 9 | An AI system applies identical rules to everyone but produces worse outcomes for one group. Is this fair? | No - Fairness requires EQUITABLE outcomes, not just equal treatment. Identical rules applied to unequal situations create unfairness. Exam tip: Equal process ≠ Fair outcomes. Watch for 'objective' systems with biased results. |
| 10 | Transparency principle - what must users understand about AI systems? | Users should understand: 1) What data is collected, 2) How the AI processes it, 3) How decisions are made. Exam tip: Transparency = understanding the system. If question asks 'should users know...?' → Transparency. |
| 11 | Which two Responsible AI principles are MOST commonly confused in exam scenarios about biased AI outputs? | Fairness (biased outcomes for different groups) vs Transparency (explaining how decisions are made). Exam tip: Biased RESULTS = Fairness. Not UNDERSTANDING decisions = Transparency. Fix fairness with better data/training, fix transparency with explanations. |
| 12 | What's the difference between Accountability and Reliability in Responsible AI? | Accountability = HUMANS are responsible (who is in charge, human review processes). Reliability = SYSTEM performs consistently (technical performance, error handling). Exam tip: 'Who is responsible?' → Accountability. 'Does it work correctly?' → Reliability. |
